## Chess

A simple browser-based chess game. Play against the computer as White or Black on Easy, Medium or Hard, or with a friend on one screen. It follows the full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, and draws by the 50-move rule, threefold repetition or insufficient material.

**[▶ Play Chess](https://ashhayama.github.io/WebApp/chess/)**

The source is in [`chess/index.html`](chess/index.html). It's a single file, so you can also download it and open it in any browser.

## 8-Ball Pool

A simple browser-based 8-ball pool game. Play against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, or with a friend on one screen. Aim with your mouse or finger, pull back to set the power and release to shoot. Sink all of your group (solids or stripes), then the 8-ball to win. Fouls give your opponent ball in hand. On a phone held upright, the table turns sideways to fit the screen.

**[▶ Play Pool](https://ashhayama.github.io/WebApp/pool/)**

The source is in [`pool/index.html`](pool/index.html). It's a single file, so you can also download it and open it in any browser.
