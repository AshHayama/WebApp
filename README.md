# WebApp

## Othello

A simple browser-based Othello game. Play against the computer or with a friend on one screen.

**[▶ Play Othello](https://ashhayama.github.io/WebApp/othello/)**

The source is in [`othello/index.html`](othello/index.html). It's a single file, so you can also download it and open it in any browser.

## Air Hockey

A simple browser-based air hockey game. Play against the computer with your mouse or finger, on Easy, Medium or Hard. First to 7 wins.

**[▶ Play Air Hockey](https://ashhayama.github.io/WebApp/air-hockey/)**

The source is in [`air-hockey/index.html`](air-hockey/index.html). It's a single file, so you can also download it and open it in any browser.

## Chess

A simple browser-based chess game. Play against the computer as White or Black on Easy, Medium or Hard, or with a friend on one screen. It follows the full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, and draws by the 50-move rule, threefold repetition or insufficient material.

**[▶ Play Chess](https://ashhayama.github.io/WebApp/chess/)**

The source is in [`chess/index.html`](chess/index.html). It's a single file, so you can also download it and open it in any browser.
