8-Ball Pool

Sink your group, then the 8-ball

Mode
Difficulty
Aim: point at the side of the cue ball you want to strike from; the guide line shows where it will go.
Shoot: press, pull back away from the cue ball to add power, then release. Release without pulling to cancel.
Fouls (scratch, missing, or hitting the wrong ball first) give your opponent ball in hand: drag the cue ball anywhere and release to place it.
